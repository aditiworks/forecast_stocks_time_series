Method, data & limitations

All prices are seeded synthetic daily close prices in USD for fictional stocks. They are generated locally with trend, noise, and optional cycles or a regime change. No live market feed or external libraries are used.

The last H sessions are held out. Each model sees only the preceding W sessions; its entire H-step prediction is produced before evaluation. MAE is the mean absolute difference between forecast and held-out prices. RMSE penalizes larger errors. All four models use the same split.

Drift extends the mean daily price change. The baseline repeats the final training price. The moving-average model repeats the mean of the last 10 training prices. Holt's model uses fixed smoothing parameters α = 0.35 and β = 0.12.

Scenario bands are a teaching approximation: forecast ± z × training change standard deviation × √h. Nominal percentages assume independent Gaussian price changes and do not guarantee empirical coverage for these models or for real stocks. The 3D paths add cumulative Gaussian noise to the forecast; they illustrate possible paths rather than additional forecasts.

There are no trading signals here. This educational demo cannot establish that a method will predict real stock prices. One test split is insufficient evidence of reliable performance.
